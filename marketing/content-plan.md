# Hitbox Website Content Plan — v2 (Patent-Informed)

## Narrative Arc

The site tells the story of a collectibles company that's **redesigning what collectibles are** — not just toys and hobbies, but **real investments** powered by NFC, blockchain, and an evolving ecosystem.

### Act 1 — "Collectibles Reimagined" (The Reveal)
**Theme:** Establishing Hitbox as a paradigm shift — from static collectibles to verified smart assets.

| Beat | Headline | Subtext | Position |
|------|----------|---------|----------|
| 1A (Hero) | HITBOX | Collectibles that live, evolve, and invest in you | Center |
| 1B | Not Toys. Real Assets. | NFC-embedded, blockchain-verified. One tap proves authenticity. | Left |
| 1C | Tap. Verify. Own It Forever. | Embedded NFC chips link physical items to on-chain identity. | Right |
| 1D | But That's Just the Beginning | What if your collectibles could grow? | Center |

### Interstitial 1 — Verification Stats
- 100% NFC Verified
- 0 Counterfeits
- 5 Evolution Tiers
- 24/7 On-Chain Proof

### Act 2 — "Living Collectibles" (The Deconstruction)
**Theme:** Trade-driven evolution, Ownership DNA, and dynamic unlockable content.

| Beat | Headline | Subtext | Position |
|------|----------|---------|----------|
| 2A | They Evolve | Trade them. Watch them transform. | Center |
| 2B | Trade-Driven Evolution | Every trade rewrites the collectible — new visuals, rarity shifts, hidden content. | Left |
| 2C | Ownership DNA | Full on-chain history — who held it, where it's been, what it unlocked. | Right |
| 2D | Collect. Trade. Level Up. | The more you play, the more your collection becomes. | Center |

### Interstitial 2 — Ecosystem Features (3 Cards)
1. **Smart Trading** (AI-Powered) — AI matches perfect trade partners, fairness scoring, auto-trade
2. **XP Seasons** (Gamified) — Earn XP through trades and engagement, seasonal leaderboards
3. **Artist Vaults** (Creator-First) — Exclusive drops, verified collectors, direct relationships

### Act 3 — "The Ecosystem" (The Reassembly)
**Theme:** P2P trading, rarity-gated access, community, and the future of collecting.

| Beat | Headline | Subtext | Position |
|------|----------|---------|----------|
| 3A | Built to Last | An economy designed for collectors | Center |
| 3B | Tap to Trade | P2P ownership transfer through NFC, no intermediary, no fees | Left |
| 3C | Rarity Unlocks Everything | Rare items = presale drops, exclusive content, VIP events | Right |
| 3D (CTA) | The Future Collects Different | Verified. Evolving. Yours. | Center |

## Design System
- **Kinetic Etherealism**: Deep indigo surfaces (#0D1117), aqua primary (#7DD8FF), teal secondary (#80DEEA), rose tertiary (#FFAFD7)
- **Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Effects**: Glassmorphism nav, gradient text animations, ambient glow (no drop shadows), tonal layering (no 1px borders)
- **Scroll animation**: 3 canvas sequences at 361 frames each, 400vh per act
